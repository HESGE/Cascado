Original procedural audio for Cascado

Files: safe-tick.wav, victory.wav, defeat.wav
Generator: scripts/generate-sounds.mjs

These sounds are original procedural synthesis and short, simple compositions
created for this project. No third-party recordings, samples or musical scores
were used. Rebuilding the generator produces the same PCM audio without metadata.
